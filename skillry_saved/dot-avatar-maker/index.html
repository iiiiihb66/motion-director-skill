FAQ

Dot avatar maker FAQ

Is the dot avatar maker free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no sign-up, no watermark and no usage limit.

What can I download?

Two formats: a 1024 × 1024 transparent PNG without background or floor shadow, and a single HTML file that shows your character full screen in interactive 3D.

Can I use my dot avatar as a profile picture?

Yes. The PNG works as a profile picture, a sticker, a chatbot avatar or an image in your slides and posts.

Is this the official OpenAI Dots avatar editor?

No. It is an independent tool from Skillry for making dot-style 3D characters, and it is not affiliated with OpenAI.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Drag with one finger to rotate your character. On phones the long-plush fur uses fewer strands so it stays smooth.

Are my characters uploaded anywhere?

No. The 3D rendering and both downloads happen in your browser.

Why does the HTML download need an internet connection?

The file stays small because it loads the open-source three.js 3D library from a public CDN when you open it. Everything else is inside the file.

About this Skill

Bring rough notes and get back a deck where every slide is composed like a board pinned to a studio wall: oversized type, offset color planes and ruled lines instead of rows of identical cards. It suits concept pitches, creative direction and workshop write-ups that a room needs to react to.

What you get

  • Boards with their own jobs, from a mounted cover and a signal map to a process wall, a cropped quote and a closing field note
  • A full plan to approve before anything is built (title, story summary, slide order and look), then the choice to refine it or branch a second direction to compare
  • A deck you can edit in your browser and export as an editable PowerPoint file or a PDF

How to use

  1. Share your notes, audience and the decision you're after. You are asked once for anything missing, with a clearly marked default if you are short on time.
  2. Approve the plan. Then ask it to refine that direction without touching unrelated slides, or branch a second one to compare side by side.
  3. Open the deck and press E to retype text, replace a picture, reorder, duplicate or skip slides, or switch between three color themes.
  4. Export a PowerPoint file or a PDF from the editor, or ask your agent. Text and speaker notes stay editable; backgrounds and diagrams arrive as pictures, and fonts are swapped for close system fonts.

Slides are in English unless you ask for another language.

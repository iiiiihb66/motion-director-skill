About this Skill

Turn your material into a yellow sketchnote presentation. The pages combine white pages with a consistent rhythm that suits small team experiment guide.

What you get

  • A complete original example and visual rules for presentations at your requested length.
  • Reusable page layouts you can select, reorder, adapt and extend to fit your material.
  • A presentation you can navigate in your browser, with an overview, speaker notes and an included print stylesheet.

How to use it

  1. Provide your topic, audience, desired slide count and the key points you want people to remember. If you have not chosen a count, discuss one based on your material and presentation time.
  2. Choose a named theme and review a page sequence that matches your requested count before filling in the details.
  3. Replace the example text with your own material, then review each page at presentation size.
  4. Add original generated artwork where the content and composition call for it, without a fixed image quota or repeating pictures to fill pages.

The included example demonstrates the style; its length does not set a default or a limit for your presentation. It uses a fictional scenario and newly created imagery. Keep the visual rules when changing the subject so the pages continue to feel like one presentation.

New image generation may have a separate provider cost. The included example opens without generating more images.

Edit and export

The deck opens in a built-in editing studio. Change text, swap images, reorder slides and switch themes in the browser, then export an editable PowerPoint file or a PDF. In PowerPoint, text and speaker notes stay editable; backgrounds and decorations come across as pictures.

What you need before you start

You bring
Presentation topic and audience · Key messages or source material · Desired slide count and language
Dependencies
A browser for presentation and verified print export · Node.js for local validation · A host image generation tool for fresh artwork · Chrome, Chromium or Edge plus Node.js 20 or newer — required only for PowerPoint export
Outside costs
Generating new artwork may incur a separate provider charge. The included original example can be viewed without image generation.

About this Skill

Build an admin console a team could adopt as-is: a calm, near-monochrome shell where colour is saved for data, a slim sidebar where every item opens a real view, and charts and tables that respond. The example is Corvane, a fictional workspace analytics product.

What you get

  • Seven views: overview, analytics, finance, customers, roadmap board, calendar and settings
  • A two-group sidebar with every item routed, a command palette on Ctrl or Cmd K, and light and dark modes in slate or tangerine
  • Tables with sort, filters, search, pagination, bulk actions and undo, plus a record drawer and dialogs
  • A drag-and-drop roadmap, a month calendar with agenda and settings forms that save
  • One file with fonts and portraits embedded that opens offline in any browser

How to use it

  1. Share your product name, the four metrics you watch, your team and what your tables, cards and events hold.
  2. Your agent replaces every number, row and event, keeps the sidebar short and reviews each view in both modes.
  3. Open the single file, try the palette, drag a card and ask for changes in plain words.

Actions a single file cannot perform, such as export or sign-out, show a clear notice instead of pretending.

What you need before you start

You bring
Product name and the four overview metrics that matter · Your team (four to eight people) and what your tables, board cards and calendar events hold · Optional: real figures, currency and team photos; otherwise sample data is drafted for you
Dependencies
Node.js 18 or newer on your agent's machine, to run the bundled script that builds the single offline file · Internet access while the file is built, so the fonts can be downloaded once and embedded · Optional: an image generation tool in your agent for a new team portrait series (the shipped portraits can stand in)
Outside costs
Team portraits can reuse the eight shipped stand-ins at no cost. Generating a new portrait series with your agent's own image tool may incur that tool's usage charges.

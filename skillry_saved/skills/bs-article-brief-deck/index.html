About this Skill

Hand it an article you have verified and it builds a briefing deck that walks your audience through the piece: an opening thesis, the argument in a few clear beats, evidence slides and a closing slide that opens the discussion. Every figure and quote on screen traces back to material you approved.

What you get

  • A paged deck with an argument spine, evidence slides and a discussion close, plus three to five talking points per slide in the speaker notes
  • Facts, quotes and labels that match what you approved, with anything unconfirmed left visibly marked as pending
  • A deck you can edit in the browser and export to an editable PowerPoint file or a PDF

How to use

  1. Share the article, its reference links, your audience and goal, and how many slides you want.
  2. Say which claims, quotes, figures and images are cleared for use.
  3. Press E to change wording, swap a picture, reorder slides or switch between the Field Note, Night Review and High Contrast themes.
  4. Export a PowerPoint file or PDF when it reads right.

What you need before you start

You bring
Approved article and source links · Audience, objective, duration, and target slide count · Claims, data, quotes, and images approved for use · Theme preset, disclosure rules, and required speaker notes
Dependencies
FAL account — optional for generated imagery · Node.js 20 or newer — required · Chrome, Chromium or Edge plus Node.js 20 or newer — required only for PowerPoint export
Outside costs
The deck works with built-in treatments. Optional image generation uses one paid FAL task; its request ID is saved and a failure is never retried automatically.

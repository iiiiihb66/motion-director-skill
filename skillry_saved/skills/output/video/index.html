How to make a video with a Skill

Three steps in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. No design app needed.

  1. 1

    Paste the install prompt

    Copy the install prompt into your agent, or run the command below. The Skill lands in your agent's skills folder.

    npx skillry-cli@latest install bs-hyperframes-code-change-launch
  2. 2

    Call it with a slash

    Type a slash and the Skill name, then say what you want in plain words.

    /bs-hyperframes-code-change-launch Create a 30-second product launch film with a strong hook and clear reveal.
  3. 3

    Review and ship

    Open the file it made, ask for changes in the same chat, then publish or hand it off.

    video.mp4

Video Skills FAQ

Can I make an AI video from text with these Skills?

Yes. Give your agent a script, a brief or a product link, and the Skill plans the shots and renders the video. Some Skills use a paid service for voice, music or AI footage, and the detail page says which.

Does every video Skill include voiceover and music?

No. Voiceover, captions, music and sound effects differ from Skill to Skill, so check each one in the detail page's You get list.

Will I get an editable project or just an MP4?

It depends on the Skill. You get an editable project only when the detail page lists project or source files; otherwise expect a rendered MP4.

Will my video look exactly like the preview?

No. The preview shows the style and the production quality. Your script, footage, length and the model's output all change the final cut.

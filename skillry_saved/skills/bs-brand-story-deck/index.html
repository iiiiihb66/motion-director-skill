What you get

  • A brand story told in order, from origin and friction to belief, visual codes, expression and future, with one clear statement on each slide and speaker notes throughout.
  • Magazine-like pages in condensed display type and a readable serif, such as a manifesto slide or a visual-codes page that sets out palette, type and motif.
  • A deck you can edit in the browser, from wording and pictures to slide order and color preset, that exports to an editable PowerPoint file with speaker notes, or to PDF.

Good fit

  • Brand designers explaining an identity rationale or the thinking behind a visual system.
  • Marketers and product teams shaping a positioning story or launch narrative.
  • Teams that want to turn a generic brand presentation into a distinctive, magazine-like sequence.

Not for

  • Inventing testimonials, metrics, founding details or customer facts; it uses only the proof you approve.
  • Data dashboards or standard pitch templates built from rows of identical cards.
  • Generating photos or reusing stock logos; graphics are drawn from shapes and type, and photos come only from images you are cleared to use.

About this Skill

Give it an approved brand brief and it turns the brand's origin, tension and belief into a bold, magazine-like story deck. Each slide makes one statement, set in big condensed headlines and a readable serif, with a clipped-corner motif that carries from cover to close.

What you get

  • A story that moves from origin and friction to belief, visual codes, expression and future
  • Pages that change their weight, from a masthead cover to a manifesto and a visual-codes page, instead of rows of identical cards
  • A deck you can edit in the browser and export to an editable PowerPoint file or a PDF

How to use

  1. Tell it who the audience is and the occasion, and share the brand's origin, tension, belief, promise and approved proof.
  2. Add voice cues, identity assets you can use and a rough length.
  3. Press E to rewrite a line, replace a picture, reorder slides or try another of the four color presets.
  4. Export a PowerPoint file or PDF when the story lands.

What you need before you start

You bring
Audience and presentation occasion · Brand origin, tension, belief, promise, and approved proof · Voice cues and identity assets approved for use
Dependencies
Modern browser — required · Node.js 20 or newer — required for local validation · Chrome, Chromium or Edge plus Node.js 20 or newer — required only for PowerPoint export

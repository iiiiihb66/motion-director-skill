About this Skill

Describe your product and get back a startup landing page with personality: warm orange-gold light beams fall through the hero and spark against a framed product dashboard, the navbar condenses into a floating pill, and the footer signs off with your name in giant letters. The example is Solvine, a fictional AI scheduling assistant for busy teams.

What you get

  • A landing page with hero, customer logos, feature bento, setup walkthrough, focus story, customer story, testimonials, security, pricing, FAQ and a trial or waitlist call to action
  • Companion pages for sign-up, sign-in, demo, early access, changelog, privacy and terms, plus a designed 404
  • Product interface drawn in code and renamed to your product: dashboard, request pipeline, inbox, globe and insight chart
  • Light and dark themes, editorial photos and embedded fonts in one file that opens offline

How to use it

  1. Share your product name, what it does, four capabilities and your plans; rough notes are enough.
  2. Your agent renames every panel to your product's vocabulary, adds photos and checks both themes on desktop and phone.
  3. Open the single file, click through the pages and ask for changes in plain words.

Photos made with your agent's image tool may incur that tool's usage charges; without one, designed panels and monogram avatars take their place.

What you need before you start

You bring
Product name, what it does and for whom, and the one outcome it promises · Four capabilities, three setup steps and two to four plans with real limits · Optional: FAQ answers, customer quotes, story metrics and photos you may use
Dependencies
Node.js 18 or newer on your agent's machine, to run the bundled script that builds the single offline file · Internet access while the file is built, so the fonts can be downloaded once and embedded · An image generation tool in your agent for editorial photos and portraits (optional; designed panels are used without it)
Outside costs
Editorial photos and portraits are generated with your agent's own image tool, which may incur that tool's usage charges. Without an image tool, designed panels and monogram avatars are used instead.

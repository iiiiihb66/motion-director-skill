About this Skill

Describe your banking or treasury product and get a dark, precise marketing site where the product UI does the selling. A restrained split hero sits beside a balance overview, and every path a finance lead needs, from opening an account to booking a demo, actually works. The site arrives as one file that opens offline in any browser.

What you get

  • Four platform tabs with working views: live balances, payment approvals, FX conversion with a rate lock and a 13-week forecast
  • A panoramic cash dashboard with a currency switch, a dotted world map of accounts, a customer story, a security bento and a developer section
  • Pricing with a billing switch and a comparison table, an FAQ and a sign-up band
  • Open-account, sign-in, demo booking, security, customer story, changelog and legal pages, with validated forms where they apply
  • Near-black surfaces, hairline rails and one silver accent, with fonts and photos embedded so the page makes no network requests

How to use it

  1. Give the Skill your product name, promise, features, currencies and rails, pricing and the security facts you can stand behind.
  2. It rebuilds every product view around your accounts and numbers, drafts any gaps and lists them for you to confirm.
  3. Open the file, click through every tab, form and page, and ask for changes in plain words.

New photos come from your agent's image tool, which may have its own usage charges.

What you need before you start

You bring
Product name, a one-line promise and who it serves · Account, payment, FX and forecasting features, currencies and rails, and your pricing tiers · Security facts you can confirm (partner banks, certifications, controls) and the main call to action
Dependencies
Node.js 18 or newer on your agent's machine, to run the bundled script that builds the single offline file · Internet access while the file is built, so the fonts can be downloaded once and embedded · An image tool in your agent for the few photos (customer story, avatars, demo host); optional if you supply your own
Outside costs
Photos you do not supply are generated with your agent's own image tool, which may incur that tool's usage charges. Product views are built in code, and the finished page makes no network requests. Viewing the example costs nothing.

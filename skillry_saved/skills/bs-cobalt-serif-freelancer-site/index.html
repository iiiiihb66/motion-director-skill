About this Skill

Turn your services, plans and projects into a designer site that sells. The hero puts you inside the headline: two italic serif words above one giant cobalt word, split by your blue-toned portrait, with a header pill that really switches light and dark. The whole site, from Services to Terms, arrives as one file that opens offline in any browser.

What you get

  • Home with a full cobalt band where white service pills drop and settle into a pile, a pricing grid, selected work, a process strip and kind words
  • Services with starting prices, Pricing with one page per plan and a side-by-side comparison, and Work with filters, search and six case studies
  • Case studies that show product screens drawn in phone and browser frames over each project image
  • About, Process, Contact with a validated brief form and intro-call slots, Terms and a designed 404
  • Schibsted Grotesk and Newsreader Italic embedded with every image, so the page makes no network requests

How to use it

  1. Give the Skill your name, discipline, the hero words, your services, two to four plans, a few projects and your contact details.
  2. It fills every page, drafts anything missing in a confident first-person voice and lists those drafts for you to confirm.
  3. Open the file in both themes, click through every page and ask for changes in plain words.

Your portrait and any new project images come from you or your agent's image tool, which may have its own usage charges.

What you need before you start

You bring
Your name, a one-word wordmark, your discipline, two italic hero words and one cobalt word · Six to twelve services with prices, and two to four plans with price, timeline and inclusions · Projects with client, role, problem, approach and result, plus contact details and availability
Dependencies
Node.js 18 or newer on your agent's machine, to run the bundled script that builds the single offline file · Internet access while the file is built, so the fonts can be downloaded once and embedded · An image tool in your agent for your portrait, project images and studio photos; optional if you supply your own
Outside costs
Images you do not supply are generated with your agent's own image tool, which may incur that tool's usage charges. Neutral studio photos ship with the Skill, case-study screens are drawn in code, and the finished page makes no network requests. Viewing the example costs nothing.

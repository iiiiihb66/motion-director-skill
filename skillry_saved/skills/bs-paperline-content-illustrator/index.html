What you get

  • A finished editorial illustration that turns one content idea into one clear visual metaphor
  • A locked visual recipe: airy black ink, subtle halftone texture, warm paper, and one restrained accent color
  • Four composition modes for article headers, social cards, course visuals, and simple explainers
  • A repeatable series workflow that carries the same visual language into later images

Good fit

  • Writers and newsletter creators who need a distinctive image without commissioning a full illustration system
  • Content marketers building a recognizable visual series across articles and social posts
  • Educators who want a simple metaphor to make an abstract idea easier to understand

Not for

  • Photorealistic scenes, glossy 3D art, or detailed painterly illustration
  • Dense infographics, long labels, or layouts where every word must be rendered inside the generated image
  • Requests to copy a named brand, illustrator, character, or supplied composition

About this Skill

Cadence Marketing Landing builds a dark SaaS marketing site where the product itself is the hero image. A charcoal ground, one cyan accent and grey-to-white gradient headlines carry one continuous story from the first screen to sign-up, with a changelog, customer story, sign-in and legal pages behind it. The example is Loopwright, a fictional customer-feedback platform.

What you get

  • A home page in one flow: hero product card, wordmark marquee, animated feature cards, a scroll-driven workflow stage, a customer spotlight with portraits, integrations and security, pricing, FAQ and a sign-up section with a laptop whose lid opens on scroll
  • Supporting pages: a filterable changelog, a customer story with three attributed numbers, sign-in and legal
  • Product screens built into the page and re-themed to your product, so one flagship request travels through the whole story
  • A working billing switch, compare table, FAQ, tabs, mobile menu and forms with validation and success states
  • Geist and Geist Mono type and generated portraits, embedded in one file that opens in any browser and works offline

How to use it

  1. Share your product name, what it does, your main customer workflow and rough pricing; notes are enough.
  2. Your agent maps the feature cards and workflow to your product, generates the portraits and fills every section and page.
  3. Open the file in your browser, click through each page and form, and ask for copy or section changes in plain words.

Portraits come from your agent's image tool when one is available, which may carry that tool's usage charges. Anything the brief leaves out becomes placeholder copy that the agent lists for you to confirm.

What you need before you start

You bring
Product name, what it does and an outcome-first headline idea · 4 to 5 capabilities, the main workflow steps and rough pricing tiers · Customer quotes, story numbers, integrations and security facts you can honestly claim (optional; gaps become listed placeholders)
Dependencies
Node.js 18 or newer on your agent's machine, to run the bundled script that builds the single offline file · Internet access while the file is built, so the fonts can be downloaded once and embedded · An image tool in your agent for the customer portraits and story photos (optional; without it the page keeps initials avatars)
Outside costs
Customer portraits and story photos are generated with your agent's own image tool when one is available, which may incur that tool's usage charges. Without one, the page keeps initials avatars and the agent lists the slots that need photos. Nothing else uses a paid service.

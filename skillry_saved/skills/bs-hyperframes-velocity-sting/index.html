What you get

  • A 10 to 13 second MP4 in your chosen canvas — square, vertical, or wide — moving through a lockup, an entry surface, a number, a navigation, one action taken, and an endcard.
  • A single-file HyperFrames project with no build step or sub-compositions, plus a seam ledger naming every cut's axis and direction, so rebranding does not break the motion.
  • The two disciplines that make it read as one camera move: one ease family for every arrival and its mirror for every exit, and the rule that nothing is allowed to sit still at a cut.

Good fit

  • You want a sting — a social post, a launch-day loop, a conference bumper, the top ten seconds of a longer film.
  • Your product has real surfaces to show: a sign-in, a number people quote, a navigation, a create button.
  • You expect to re-brand it later, or to make a second one that matches the first.

Not for

  • Explaining what your product does. There is no room, and the piece is a texture rather than an argument.
  • Products with no navigation and no create affordance — two of the seven slots would have to be invented, and it shows.
  • Anyone who wants to dress the film with a competitor's or partner's interface. Every surface here is yours or a neutral original.

How to build a brand site with a Skill

Three steps in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. No design app needed.

  1. 1

    Paste the install prompt

    Copy the install prompt into your agent, or run the command below. The Skill lands in your agent's skills folder.

    npx skillry-cli@latest install bs-hero-minimal-motion
  2. 2

    Call it with a slash

    Type a slash and the Skill name, then say what you want in plain words.

    /bs-hero-minimal-motion Develop three brand identity directions from this positioning brief.
  3. 3

    Review and ship

    Open the file it made, ask for changes in the same chat, then publish or hand it off.

    index.html

Why use a brand identity Skill instead of a prompt?

A prompt can make one on-brand image. A Skill keeps your colors, type and tone steady across a brand site, a brand story deck and a short film, so people recognize you wherever they see you.

Brand identity Skills FAQ

Can AI build a brand website from my existing logo and colors?

Yes. Most brand site Skills ask for your logo, color values, fonts and a few references, then build around them instead of inventing a new look. The Skill page lists what to prepare.

Is there a Skill that writes brand guidelines or a brand kit?

Not as a standalone document. The Skills here put your existing guidelines to work on real pieces: brand sites, brand story decks, posters and short brand films. If your brand isn't settled yet, a brand story deck is a good place to pin down positioning and visual codes.

Where do I find single brand images, like a poster?

If you only need one finished visual, browse image Skills. Stay on this page when several pieces need to match.

Do I get editable files or just images?

Most Skills on this page deliver editable source, such as HTML pages or slides you can open and change. Each Skill page lists the exact files you get.

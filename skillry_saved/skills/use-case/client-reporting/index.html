Client reporting Skills FAQ

Can AI turn a spreadsheet into a client report?

Yes. Give your agent the spreadsheet or CSV, say how each metric is defined and which date range to use, and a chart or report deck Skill builds from it. Keep those definitions in the brief so the figures stay exact.

Should a QBR or monthly report be a deck or a chart page?

Use a deck when you present it live and want a decision in the room, and a chart page when the client reads it alone. For slide formats, browse presentation Skills.

Will the Skill check my numbers?

No, so don't rely on it. Reconcile the final figures against your source system before the report goes out.

Is it safe to use confidential client data?

Share only what the report needs and follow your client agreement. Remove names and identifiers where you can, and check whether any tool the Skill calls sends data to an outside service.

Three steps in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. No design app needed.

    Paste the install prompt

Social media Skills FAQ

Can AI make social media posts in one consistent style?

Yes. A social Skill fixes the layout, fonts and colors, so every quote card or share image you make with it looks like part of the same series. You change the words and the style stays put.

Is there a Skill for YouTube thumbnails or Instagram carousels?

Not right now. The Skills here make OG share images, quote and insight cards, testimonial walls, posters and short videos. Many of those work as single-image posts on Instagram, LinkedIn or X.

Does the Skill post to my accounts?

Usually not. It makes the image or video file, and you post, schedule and reply from your own accounts unless the Skill page names a platform integration.

Where are the Skills for short social videos?

For short clips where motion and sound do most of the work, browse video Skills.

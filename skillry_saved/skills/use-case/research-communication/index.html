Research Skills FAQ

Can AI make a scientific poster from my paper?

Yes. Give your agent the final paper and the conference's poster requirements, and a poster Skill lays out the question, methods, results and figures. Check every number and caption against the paper before you print.

Will the Skill verify my citations and statistics?

No. Check each citation against its source, and have your team re-run any calculation that matters.

Should I present my research as slides or a poster?

Slides suit a talk where you walk people through the argument, while a poster or figure has to make its case on its own. If the venue wants a talk, compare presentation Skills.

Can I use unpublished or sensitive research?

Only within your institution's rules, consent terms and any embargo. Before you share restricted material, check where each tool the Skill uses stores or sends your files.

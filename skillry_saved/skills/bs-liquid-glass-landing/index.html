About this Skill

Turn a name and one promise into a dark landing page that feels like frosted glass over a film: glass pills that catch light, a huge serif headline with one italic word, and an email capture front and centre. The worked example is the fictional newsletter Nightfold, with a live email preview, membership pricing and seven working routes. The default result is one self-contained file that opens offline in any browser.

What you get

  • A glass pill header, a cinematic hero with email capture, a live email preview with chart, a facts strip and a method split
  • An editions picker, editors, reader notes, membership pricing with a monthly or yearly toggle, recent editions, FAQ and a closing capture with countdown
  • Sample edition, archive, charter, join, sign-in and legal pages, plus a designed 404
  • Instrument Serif, Newsreader, Geist and Geist Mono embedded, with zero network requests

How to use it

  1. Share your name, what the product is, what signing up gets people, the cadence, prices and the people behind it.
  2. Your agent fills the sections, renders your product as a live screen in the glass panel and creates dark images or uses your hero footage.
  3. Open the page, try the capture forms, pricing toggle and archive filters, then ask for changes.

Generated images may incur your image tool's usage charges. The forms confirm in the page and send nothing until you connect your own mailing service.

What you need before you start

You bring
Name, what the product is and what signing up gets the visitor · Cadence or launch date, prices or tiers, a few honest facts and the people behind it · Optional: your own dark hero footage and team photos
Dependencies
Node.js 18 or newer on your agent's machine, to run the bundled script that builds the single offline file · Internet access while the file is built, so the fonts can be downloaded once and embedded · Optional: an image generation tool in your agent; without one, media frames get dark placeholder surfaces
Outside costs
Generating the dark images with your agent's image tool may incur that tool's usage charges. Without an image tool the page still builds, with dark placeholder surfaces in each media frame.

About this Skill

Bring your approved analysis and it builds a consulting-style deck in which every slide title states its point, from the first finding to the decision you need. Clean pages, restrained red accents and structured evidence blocks keep assumptions and decision gates in plain view.

What you get

  • An action-title storyline that runs from executive finding through evidence to recommendation, roadmap and steering decision
  • Evidence slides with headline metrics and the assumptions behind them, plus three to five talking points per slide in the speaker notes
  • A deck you can edit in the browser and export to an editable PowerPoint file or a PDF

How to use

  1. Share the client brief, the decision you want, your verified analysis and metrics, and the meeting context.
  2. Say how many slides you need.
  3. Press E to tighten a title, swap a picture, reorder slides or switch between the Redline Advisory, Blueproof and Green Value themes.
  4. Export a PowerPoint file or PDF for the meeting.

What you need before you start

You bring
Approved client brief and decision objective · Verified analysis, metrics, and source notes · Meeting context and target slide count · Rights-cleared charts or optional generated-image brief
Dependencies
FAL account — optional for generated imagery · Node.js 20 or newer — required · Chrome, Chromium or Edge plus Node.js 20 or newer — required only for PowerPoint export
Outside costs
The deck works with built-in treatments. Optional image generation uses paid FAL tasks; each request ID is saved and failures are never retried automatically.

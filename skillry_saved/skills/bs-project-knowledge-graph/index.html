What you get

  • A self-contained project graph with searchable entities and directional relationships.
  • An evidence inspector that shows recorded source paths for manual lookup, lifecycle state, neighbors, and backlinks.
  • A transparent entity-relation dataset plus a documented graph-size and omission policy.

Good fit

  • Project archives spread across notes and structured records.
  • Teams tracing decisions, owners, dependencies, and unresolved work.
  • Reviewers who need every displayed claim tied to its source.

Not for

  • Replacing source-document governance or access control.
  • Claiming complete knowledge from an incomplete project archive.
  • Rendering unrestricted private data for public distribution.

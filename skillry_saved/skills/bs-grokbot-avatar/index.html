What you get

  • Two shape atlases with 16 rounded silhouette directions for Original avatars, with support for other shapes you request.
  • Eight soft-vinyl character references for cute, large-headed toy avatars with simple eyes, matte surfaces, and subtle technology details.
  • Request-first personalization: explicit character details take priority, while reliable user memory can fill missing details for your own avatar.

Good fit

  • Personal profiles with a recognizable geometric mascot or a cute soft-vinyl character.
  • Turning an approved Original mascot into an Anime avatar with the same palette and shape language.
  • Creating coordinated avatar sets with different ages, hairstyles, clothes, and character details.

Not for

  • Photorealistic portraits or detailed human anatomy as the default look.
  • Editable vector logos, character rigs, or animated avatars.
  • Inferring personal age or gender from a name, photograph, occupation, or stereotype.

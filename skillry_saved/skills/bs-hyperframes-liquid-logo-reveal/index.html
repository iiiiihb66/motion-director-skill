What you get

  • A 6 to 8 second 1920x1080 MP4: a bead of liquid gathers, carries your mark in a state you cannot quite read, holds, ruptures, and leaves your lockup crisp on the frame.
  • The editable project with the whole liquid built from four SVG filter primitives — no 3D scene, no environment map, no model file — so it renders on your own machine at no per-frame cost.
  • The tuning method that matters: the distortion is set against a legibility test at the frame before the rupture, not by eye, so the reveal always has something left to reveal.

Good fit

  • You need an outro — the last four to eight seconds of a film, an ad, or a screen loop.
  • You have your mark as a real vector file and the right to use it.
  • Your mark has a recognisable silhouette. That is what carries the whole first half.

Not for

  • Introducing or explaining anything. There is no copy in this structure before the rupture, and adding some removes the only subject in the frame.
  • Brands whose identity is a wordmark set in a system typeface — there is no silhouette to recognise under the liquid.
  • Anyone who cannot supply real logo artwork. A mark drawn from a description is wrong in ways only people who know the brand will notice.

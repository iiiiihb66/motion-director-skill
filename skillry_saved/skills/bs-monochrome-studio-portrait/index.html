What you get

  • A finished black-and-white half portrait with a soft studio gradient, one-direction lighting, subtle film grain, and deliberate negative space
  • A face-first edit recipe that locks visible facial anchors before changing the setting, wardrobe, crop, and photographic finish
  • Dedicated eyeglasses checks covering frame shape, bridge, temples, fit, scale, and readable eyes
  • A side-by-side QA pass for identity, anatomy, lighting, skin texture, grain, composition, and unwanted text or branding

Good fit

  • Editorial profiles, personal-brand pages, portfolios, and social portraits that need a quieter studio look
  • People who want an urban-casual black-and-white portrait while keeping their face and glasses recognizable
  • Photographers and creators building a visually consistent portrait series from authorized source photos

Not for

  • Passport photos, identity documents, biometric matching, or proof that someone was present in a real studio
  • Portraits made without the subject's permission or images whose usage rights are unclear
  • Guaranteed pixel-perfect identity reproduction after large pose, age, hairstyle, or body changes

About this Skill

Turn your notes into a deck that plays like a short film: one full-frame scene and one short caption per slide, from the opening image through the turning point to a closing call to action. You carry the story out loud; the screen sets the mood.

What you get

  • Full-frame scenes whose framing shifts as the story moves, from wide openings to close details and quiet pauses, with captions usually under 24 words
  • Speaker notes on every slide, with three to five talking points that add context instead of repeating the caption
  • A deck you can edit in your browser and export as an editable PowerPoint file with your notes, or as a PDF

How to use

  1. Share your topic, audience, the feeling you want to leave them with and your confirmed facts. Add photos you have the rights to, or skip them: each scene can be drawn as original artwork, with no paid image generation.
  2. Open the deck and press E to rewrite a caption, replace a picture, reorder, duplicate or skip scenes, or switch between three color themes.
  3. Export a PowerPoint file or a PDF from the editor, or ask your agent. Captions and notes stay editable; scenes arrive as pictures, and fonts are swapped for close system fonts.

Slides are in English unless you ask for another language. If key facts are missing or image rights are unclear, it pauses until you fill the gap.

What you need before you start

You bring
Topic, audience, and intended emotional arc · Approved source facts and required narrative beats · Optional rights-cleared visual references
Dependencies
Modern browser — required · Chrome, Chromium or Edge plus Node.js 20 or newer — required only for PowerPoint export

About this Skill

Turn your material into a sky glass presentation. The pages combine deep navy to sky gradient with a consistent rhythm that suits climate observatory.

What you get

  • A presentation built around your requested slide count, with an original example showing the visual style.
  • Adaptable page layouts for explanations, comparisons and a clear sequence of ideas, with imagery chosen for the content.
  • A presentation you can navigate in your browser, with an overview, speaker notes and an included print stylesheet.

How to use it

  1. Provide your topic, audience, requested slide count and the key points you want people to remember.
  2. Choose a named theme and review the page plan. Pages can be added, removed, reordered or expanded to match your requested count.
  3. Replace the example text with your own material, then review each page at presentation size.
  4. Add original generated artwork according to the content and composition of each page; there is no fixed image quota.

If you have not specified a slide count, a page plan is proposed from your content and presentation duration and confirmed with you. The included example does not set a default or limit for new presentations.

The included example uses a fictional scenario and newly created imagery. Keep the visual rules when changing the subject so the pages continue to feel like one presentation.

New image generation may have a separate provider cost. The included example opens without generating more images.

Edit and export

The deck opens in a built-in editing studio. Change text, swap images, reorder slides and switch themes in the browser, then export an editable PowerPoint file or a PDF. In PowerPoint, text and speaker notes stay editable; backgrounds and decorations come across as pictures.

What you need before you start

You bring
Presentation topic and audience · Key messages or source material · Desired slide count and language
Dependencies
A browser for presentation and verified print export · Node.js for local validation · A host image generation tool for fresh artwork · Chrome, Chromium or Edge plus Node.js 20 or newer — required only for PowerPoint export
Outside costs
Generating new artwork may incur a separate provider charge. The included original example can be viewed without image generation.

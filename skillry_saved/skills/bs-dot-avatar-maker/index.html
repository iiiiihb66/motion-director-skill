What you get

  • The studio's complete source: one self-contained HTML file, yours to rebrand, extend or build into your website or app with your AI agent.
  • 25 forms (11 classic dots, 12 animals, 2 fantasy), 12 finishes, 32 boy and girl styles, nine palette colors plus a custom picker, and 18 languages.
  • A live studio that opens in your browser, with one-click downloads: a 1024 × 1024 transparent PNG or a 3D page of your character you can spin.

Good fit

  • Developers and site owners who want a ready-made character creator to rebrand and add to their product.
  • Anyone who wants a cute avatar, sticker or mascot without drawing or prompting.
  • Creators who need a consistent set of characters in matching colors and finishes.

Not for

  • Photorealistic portraits or a likeness of a real person.
  • Animated video, rigged models or files for 3D printing.
  • Teams that need a hosted service or an API: you run and host the page yourself.

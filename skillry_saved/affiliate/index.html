Before you get started

How are referrals tracked?

PromoteKit records referrals when visitors follow your link to Skillry. We do not copy cookies from your website. Browser privacy settings or blocked cookies can affect attribution.

Is the commission recurring?

Yes. The 40% rate applies to qualifying payments within 12 months of the referred customer’s first purchase, including renewals during that period. It is not a lifetime commission.

Where do I see commissions and payouts?

Your affiliate dashboard shows your referrals and commissions. Payouts are handled manually; contact us for the current payout arrangements.
